Getting Started

Four quick steps to build your apartment layout.

  1. CalibrateOpen Scale and enter a known wall or room dimension.
  2. Add furnitureTap ＋ Furniture, enter the real dimensions, and create the piece.
  3. ArrangeDrag pieces on the plan. Turn Snap off for free placement.
  4. SaveName your arrangement in Layout and save it. Back it up to Google Drive.
